Privacy Policy

W-Vault · Effective 27 September 2026 · Last updated 27 September 2026

In short
  • W-Vault is a personal messaging tool. It receives the WhatsApp messages you send to its WhatsApp Business number so its operator can read, keep and answer them.
  • Your messages are stored encrypted. They are never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with other customers.
  • You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time by emailing raoof.r12@gmail.com. See how to delete your data.

1. Who we are

W-Vault (Meta app ID 1306158711456072) is operated by Mohammad Raouf Abedini, an individual based in Sydney, Australia ("we", "us"). W-Vault is part of the open-source comms gateway. We decide how the information described here is used, and we are responsible for it.

Contact: raoof.r12@gmail.com

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers information we receive when you message, call or interact with our WhatsApp Business number through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) operated by Meta. It does not cover WhatsApp itself. Meta's own use of your information is described in the WhatsApp Privacy Policy. W-Vault does not use Facebook Login and receives no data from Facebook or Instagram accounts.

4. How we use it

  • To communicate with you: to read and reply to your messages, and to send messages you have agreed to receive. This is necessary to provide the conversation you started, and it is our legitimate interest in answering you.
  • To keep a record of our conversations: so we can follow up and refer back to what was said.
  • To keep the service secure: for example, to confirm that each message genuinely came from WhatsApp and to reject forged ones.

In line with the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, we use information we receive about you, other than the content of our conversations, only as reasonably necessary to support messaging with you. We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

We message you only after you have given us your number and agreed to hear from us. Reply STOP, or tell us in any words, and we will stop messaging you.

6. Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Your messages are stored on the operator's own computer, in an encrypted database (SQLCipher) that only the operator can open.
  • While our computer is offline, incoming messages wait in an encrypted mailbox run on Cloudflare. They are encrypted with a key held only on our computer.
  • Every message is checked against WhatsApp's signature before we accept it, and every change we make is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.

Our service providers may process data outside Australia, including in the United States and the European Union, under their own safeguards.

7. How long we keep it

  • Messages waiting in our Cloudflare mailbox: deleted as soon as we collect them, and in any case after at most 30 days.
  • Our conversation history: kept while we have an ongoing conversation or relationship with you, and deleted when you ask or when it is no longer needed.
  • Meta's copies: Meta keeps messages for up to 30 days to operate the service, as described in Meta's documentation.

8. How to delete your data

You can ask us to delete everything we hold about you at any time:

  1. Email raoof.r12@gmail.com with the subject "W-Vault data deletion", or send the word DELETE to our WhatsApp number.
  2. Tell us the WhatsApp number you messaged us from, so we can find your data. We may ask you to confirm the request from that number.
  3. We delete your messages, media and identifiers from our records within 30 days and confirm to you when it is done.

Deleting your data does not delete the messages in your own WhatsApp app. To remove those, delete the chat in WhatsApp.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the EU and UK GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • access the information we hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive a copy of it.

To use any of these rights, email raoof.r12@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to your local privacy regulator. In Australia, that is the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

10. Children

W-Vault is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has messaged us, contact us and we will delete their information.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will tell you before it takes effect. Every earlier version is kept in the public history of this page.
